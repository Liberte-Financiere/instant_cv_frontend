[2026-03-22] — feat: add full AI-powered CV translation (EN, FR, ZH)

What changed:
- Added `AI_CV_TRANSLATE` credit cost (15 credits) in `credits.ts`.
- Created a new backend endpoint `/api/cv/[id]/translate` that leverages the `gemini-2.5-pro` model to translate a CV's JSON content exactly.
- Added a `translateCV` action in the Zustand store `useCVStore.ts` to coordinate the translation request, display toasts, and pull the new CV.
- Built a new `TranslateCVButton` component with a dropdown for selecting the target language.
- Added the translate button to the header toolbar in the CV Editor.
- Removed legacy `@page letter` named page logic in `globals.css` and `LetterPreview.tsx` that was causing extra blank pages in PDF exports.

Why:
- To allow users to instantly translate their CV for international applications without losing their formatting or structural data.
- The translation creates a new copy of the CV instead of overwriting the original, acting as a safeguard for user data.
- The named page PDF fix ensures flawless PDF generations without ghost pages at the end of cover letters.
